## Status — 11-14: two workflows

Decided with K4TEL (11-14): spreadsheets are textual; CSV, TSV and JSON are extracted as they are; an office
document keeps its native text, placed on the pages of its rendering.

| Workflow | Inputs                              | Text           | Pages, boxes              | ATR                                                 |
|----------|-------------------------------------|----------------|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| visual   | PDF                                 | PDF engine     | PDF pages                 | point 2 rules                                       |
| visual   | DOCX, DOC, ODT, RTF                 | native readers | LibreOffice rendering, pt | point 2 rules, plus rendered text the reading lacks |
| textual  | ODS, XLSX, XLS, TXT, CSV, TSV, JSON | as they are    | none                      | never                                               |

- **Rendering** (`api_util/office_render.py`):
  - The DOCX, ODT, or the DOC's converted DOCX is rendered from a copy with its tracked changes accepted, because
    LibreOffice draws deletions (checked on `rich.docx`). The copy also carries the main content type, without
    which LibreOffice will not load `bare.docx`.
  - The export options are pinned. The date, `/ID` and `/DocChecksum` are fixed in place. A rerun lays the pages
    out the same, so the docproc does not change; the PDF's bytes may still differ (the CI runner, 11-15), so its
    SHA-512 names the PDF this run handed over.
- **Placement** (`api_util/render_place.py`):
  - The native words are matched to the rendered words in reading order, with a second pass for text drawn
    elsewhere.
  - A native line is cut at the rendered line breaks and takes the box of its rendered words.
  - An unmatched line keeps its text, without a box.
  - A page whose rendered body words the text covers less than `RENDER_TEXT_COVERAGE` (0.8) is `needsAtr` (H12).
- **Hand-over:** `--rendered-out`; `/reformat` returns `rendered_pdf` when a page is routed `atr`, or with
  `rendered=true`. The paradata name it `rendered-pdf`, `stored: false`.
- **Textual:**
  - TXT keeps its lines and paragraphs, with form feeds as pages.
  - Every CSV/TSV row and every JSON string value becomes a line.
  - Lines over `MAX_LINE_CHARS` (1000) are wrapped, and each wrap is recorded as a `split`.
  - A reading that would skip content fails the run.
  - Decode flags stay on the lines, and these pages never go to the ATR.
- **Gone:** `--docx-page-breaks` and `docx_page_breaks` (the pages are the rendering's). The native DOCX reader
  reads with explicit breaks.
- **Image:** metric-compatible fonts, no hyphenation dictionary. CI installs LibreOffice for the rendering tests.
- **Checked:**
  - The suite passes, with and without LibreOffice.
  - On the fixtures, `scan_page.docx` routes only its picture page to the ATR, and `photo_text.docx` none.
  - ocr-postprocess's `build_record.py` builds the office documents' records with pt canvases and measured boxes.
    It builds the textual ones with `origin: atr` until its `DIGITAL_FORMATS` gains `txt`, `csv`, `tsv` and
    `json`.

## Status — 11-19: every cell (motyc, 11-18)

motyc on the 11-16 status: a sheet keeps every non-empty cell, numbers and dates in the form the sheet displays
them, as the CSV path keeps every column; PPTX and ODP need no change. This answers item 5 above. Decided with
K4TEL (11-19):

- **An ODS** already stores, in each cell, the text the sheet displayed: the shared reader reads it for every cell,
  whatever its type. A typed cell without that text keeps its stored value, counted (`ods_values_as_stored`).
- **An XLSX or XLS** stores raw values and number formats, so LibreOffice displays it and the ODS it makes is read.
  The locale is **cs-CZ** (`SPREADSHEET_LOCALE`), set in the run's throw-away profile. An XLSX does not say how its
  numbers and default dates read, and AMČR's sheets are read in Czech. A spike confirmed that the profile setting
  alone switches the display:

  | Cell                          | en-US (no setting) | cs-CZ              |
  |-------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
  | `0.00`, 1.5                   | `1.50`             | `1,50`             |
  | `#,##0`, 12345                | `12,345`           | `12 345`           |
  | `0%`, 0.25                    | `25%`              | `25 %`             |
  | numFmt 14 (short date), 44270 | `3/15/2021`        | `15.3.2021`        |
  | TRUE                          | `TRUE`             | `PRAVDA`           |
  | General, 3.14159265358979     | `3.14159265358979` | `3,14159265358979` |

  The paradata name the locale as the parameter `spreadsheet_locale`, and LibreOffice with its version. An XLSX now
  needs LibreOffice (`dependency_missing` without it), as an XLS did.
