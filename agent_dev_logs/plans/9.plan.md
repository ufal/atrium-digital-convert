## Where it stands — 2026-11-13

1. **Quality is scoring's** (point 1): nothing left. digital-convert's decode verdict is a docproc line flag since
   round 3; the page mean and the bands are gone.
2. **The first-pass page check** (point 2): as built. A page whose layer does not decode, is an earlier OCR run or
   has no text is `needsAtr`, with its reason, in docproc's routing; the whole document then goes through the ATR
   once.
3. **`garbled` narrowed** (point 3), done in round 4:
   - `api_util/digital_to_json.py`: a page whose words lose letters to unmapped glyphs is no longer flagged; it
     stays `digital`, its U+FFFD characters in its lines, for scoring;
   - `ROUTE_DAMAGED_WORD_SHARE` is removed (`tool_limits.py`, `.env.example`, `docs/configuration.md`, the service
     README's limits table), since routing was its only use;
   - `/describe` (parked) counts the damaged words in `layout` but routes nothing on them;
   - `README.md` defines `garbled` as broken font encodings and glyphs without a Unicode mapping;
   - tests: the `unmapped.pdf` page that loses letters is routed `text-layer`, without `needsAtr`, its text kept.
4. **Text-only inputs** (point 4): they skip the visual steps and go straight to scoring. Which tool reads them into
   the record, digital-convert (a docproc without geometry) or ocr-postprocess (its own text readers), is the hub's
   Q9; nothing changes here until it is answered.
5. **Office files rendered** (point 5), to do: render DOCX (and ODT, RTF, DOC through LibreOffice, already in the
   image) to PDF, and take the page geometry in `pt` from the rendering, so office files get page sizes and line
   boxes like a PDF. Only the pages of point 2 go to page-classification and the ATR, not every page with an
   embedded image. Then ask 13's "flow document without a page size" is needed for the text-only inputs only.
   Open for the implementation: how the rendered page is matched with the DOCX's own page breaks (`PAGE_BREAKS`).

## Boundaries

- Scoring stays ocr-postprocess's (D18); nothing here judges whether text reads.
- `/describe` stays parked (H11): it follows the routing rules, it is not extended.
- The docproc schema is ARÚP's (D30): the proposal's fields for flow documents stay until the rendering lands.
