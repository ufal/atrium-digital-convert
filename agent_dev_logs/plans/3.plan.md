## What stays binding after closure

These are the invariants a change to the renderers must keep. The tests enforce them: `tests/test_md_stress.py`, `tests/test_json_to_md.py`, `tests/test_xml_to_md.py` and `tests/test_layout_md.py`.

1. **One model-facing dialect.** The record route (`json_to_md.py`) and the TEITOK/ALTO route (`xml_to_md.py --format layout`) emit one cue vocabulary (`layout_md.py`) and compatible page labels and order, at every profile (`full`, `standard`, `minimal`).
2. **No enrichment replay.** The renderer never reads `enrichment` or any other model output as source text.
3. **Text never forges a cue** (#6).
4. **Consumers do not render their own Markdown.** keyword-extract's whole-document clients read this renderer's output and record its `CONVERTER_ID`. A change to `CONVERTER_ID` is made on both sides (`api_util/json_to_md.py`, keyword-extract `llm_client_shared.py`).
5. **Engines feed the record, not the prompt.** A parser (light, Docling, or later MinerU/Marker) produces the `atrium_document` record, so the Markdown contract does not change with the engine.
