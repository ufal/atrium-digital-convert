#### <img src="https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/65828620?u=80b39d3cb2967c52e1d4af28dcefc7a1efdfdc79&v=4" width="50">[K4TEL](https://github.com/K4TEL) commented at [2026-10-08 10:51](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-digital-convert/issues/5#issuecomment-6058256569):

Both asks checked against `v1.1.1-beta`:

- **Uploads over 100 MB.** The default limit is 50 MB (`MAX_UPLOAD_MB`); a larger file is refused with 413 `limit_exceeded` naming the setting. With `MAX_UPLOAD_MB=200` a synthetic 120 MB PDF is accepted in 4 s. The upload is held in memory and the server's peak rose by about 250 MB, so budget about twice the largest file per concurrent job. The next limits a large scan meets are `STAGE_TIMEOUT_S` (120 s) and `MAX_PAGES` (2000). Which values should the pilot use?
- **Damaged extractions before `/describe`.** A page is flagged (`needs_ocr`; `pages[].text_layer` is `none`, `garbled` or `ocr`) when it has no text layer, a layer that does not decode, or a prior OCR layer. A page with a little real text over a full-page image (a stamp, a header, only the PDF's metadata) is **not** flagged.

The 128 are 99 pages without usable text and 29 pages of CTX202000362 that lose every ř, ě, č and ů (shown as `�`): the PDF has no character for them.

**Fixed since the first files:** words broken at Czech letters, small capitals split ("O BRÁZEK"), vertical table headers read letter by letter, paragraphs marked as headings, and good pages (tables, codes, coordinates) sent to OCR by the text-quality check.

**Still imperfect:**
- Six excavation plans in CTX202000362 (pp. 37, 41, 44, 45, 47, 49) carry their labels ("Objekt 22") in the image, and these files treat them as text pages. The code now sends such pages to OCR.

- The text-quality check rates 625 born-digital pages "Trash" (tables and captions whose text is exact). It no longer decides where they go, but the rating is stored with them.

**For the AMČR cluster:** page types need 4 GB of GPU memory, and the text-quality check peaked at 23 GB while scoring, so the two do not yet fit one shared 24 GB card. A smaller batch setting will be measured.
