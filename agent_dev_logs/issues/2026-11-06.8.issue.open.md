Part of ufal/atrium-page-classification#58 (overview: ufal/atrium-project#83). For each page, `/describe` reports the text layer, page-classification's label and a route. It does not say what kind of document the page is: a report page, a site photograph, a trench plan, a map, an object drawing. A decision model can read the rendered page and its text, and pick a type from a fixed list, with a probability for each option.

## Scope

1. **Type list v0**, `decisions/document_type.v0.json`. It is generated from AMČR's heslář `dokument_typ` (keyword-extract's `data_samples/vocab/amcr_flat.json`) and records the source commit and SHA-256:
   - 32 of the 41 terms, each with its `api.aiscr.cz` URI. The 9 data-only types (geodetic, geophysical, GNSS and vector-plan data, the 3D types and others) are left out;
   - `neurčený dokument` as the option for abstaining;
   - a coarse group, asked in the same request: text, photograph, aerial photograph, plan or map, drawing, unspecified.
2. **An opt-in `document-type` stage** for `/describe` and the CLI, chosen through `--stages` / `stages`:
   - **per page:** the page rendered with pypdfium2 (long side at most 1024 px), the text layer, the layout counts and the body text;
   - **per document:** one answer, from the first 4 pages at most and the opening text;
   - the same status set and error handling as the page-classification stage.
3. **In the report only:** `PageReport.document_type` and `summary.document_type`. The record, `pages[].category` and `route_page()` do not change.
4. **Evaluation** on the 15 AMČR PDFs (2,514 pages), on the cluster, reporting counts only. It compares:
   - text only, image only, and both;
   - a flat choice (32 types) against two levels (the group, then the type);
   - the models: Clef, then Clef-Flash; Strands Decider 2B on text only.

- [ ] Each level is scored:

  - latency, for text requests and image requests separately;

- [ ] A recommendation (stop, keep in the report, or propose a record field) is posted here.

## Boundaries

- `pages[].category` and `page_categories` stay page-classification's.
- The 15 PDFs, and everything derived from them, stay on the cluster and private (#5).
- Not part of the pilot release. No hosted inference.

For Dana two requests about the 15 sample PDFs of #5 from Opus.

For each page, the model will receive the rendered page image (long side at most 1024 px), the page's text and a few layout counts. In one request it answers two questions:
- a broad group, with 6 options: text, photograph, aerial photograph, plan or map, drawing, unspecified;
- the AMČR document type, with 32 options from `dokument_typ`, each described by its English and Czech label, e.g. "fieldwork report (nálezová zpráva)".

It returns a probability for every option, so its answer is always one of the listed types, though it can be the wrong one. Each document is also answered once, from its first four pages and its opening text. The generated list: [decisions/document_type.v0.json](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-digital-convert/blob/test/decisions/document_type.v0.json).

To measure how often it is right, we need correct answers that do not come from the model:

1. **The catalogue type of each document:** could you send the `dokument_typ` the AMČR catalogue records for each of the 15 documents, by CTX id?
2. **Page labels:** who at AMČR or ARÚP/ARÚB could label about 300 of their pages, each with one of the 32 types, from written definitions? 50 of the pages would be labelled by two people, to see how often they agree.

P.S.

Two changes to what the model receives, released in [`v1.4.7-beta`](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-digital-convert/releases/tag/v1.4.7-beta):

- By default it sees only the rendered page. `--with-text` adds the page's text layer, so the two can be compared on the same pages.
- The list is now [`decisions/document_type.v2.json`](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-digital-convert/blob/test/decisions/document_type.v2.json), with one view per input. PDF pages get the 6 groups and 32 types. keyword-extract reads OCR text only, so it gets only the 6 written types and "unspecified".

`tools/decide_documents.py` answers per page and per document, and scores both against catalogue types (`--gold`) and page labels (`--page-gold`) once they exist. Offline only; nothing has been run on a model yet.

#### <img src="https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/65828620?u=80b39d3cb2967c52e1d4af28dcefc7a1efdfdc79&v=4" width="50">[K4TEL](https://github.com/K4TEL) commented at [2026-11-08 17:30](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-digital-convert/issues/8#issuecomment-6724712341):

**Decision models, briefly.** A decision model is a language model that chooses instead of writing. It gets an input (a page image, a text, or both) and questions whose answers are fixed in advance: yes/no, one option from a list, or a point on a scale. It returns a probability for every allowed answer, so it never answers off the list, though it can choose wrongly. It is not trained on our data: a short task description, the question and one line per answer are all it is told. One pass answers dozens of questions, so it is cheap, and the probabilities mark the cases it is unsure of, which a person or the existing tool can then decide.

How its answers are read and measured, beside accuracy against the catalogue types and the labelled pages:
- **"Unspecified"** is one of the 32 types: the model's way of saying it cannot tell. How often it says so is reported.
- **Group and type** are answered side by side, so a "plan or map" group with a photograph type is a contradiction, counted without any gold. With gold, a wrong type is also checked for landing at least in the right group.
- **Documents** are answered in a request of their own, never assembled from the page answers, so the two levels can be compared for reliability.
