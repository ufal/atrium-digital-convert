Part of ufal/atrium-page-classification#58 (overview: ufal/atrium-project#83). For each page, `/describe` reports the text layer, page-classification's label and a route. It does not say what kind of document the page is: a report page, a site photograph, a trench plan, a map, an object drawing. A decision model can read the rendered page and its text, and pick a type from a fixed list, with a probability for each option.

## Scope

1. **Type list v0**, `decisions/document_type.v0.json`. It is generated from AMČR's heslář `dokument_typ` (keyword-extract's `data_samples/vocab/amcr_flat.json`) and records the source commit and SHA-256:
   - 32 of the 41 terms, each with its `api.aiscr.cz` URI. The 9 data-only types (geodetic, geophysical, GNSS and vector-plan data, the 3D types and others) are left out;
   - `neurčený dokument` as the option for abstaining;
   - a coarse group, asked in the same request: text, photograph, aerial photograph, plan or map, drawing, unspecified.
2. **An opt-in `document-type` stage** for `/describe` and the CLI, chosen through `--stages` / `stages`:
   - **per page:** the page rendered with pypdfium2 (long side at most 1024 px), the text layer, the layout counts and the body text;
   - **per document:** one answer, from the first 4 pages at most and the opening text;
   - the same status set and error handling as the page-classification stage.
3. **In the report only:** `PageReport.document_type` and `summary.document_type`. The record, `pages[].category` and `route_page()` do not change.
4. **Evaluation** on the 15 AMČR PDFs (2,514 pages), on the cluster, reporting counts only. It compares:
   - text only, image only, and both;
   - a flat choice (32 types) against two levels (the group, then the type);
   - the models: Clef, then Clef-Flash; Strands Decider 2B on text only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AMČR's answer on the list is recorded on ufal/atrium-project#83:
  - is `dokument_typ` the right list?
  - should the 9 data types stay out?
  - can each sample PDF's catalogue type serve as gold at the document level?
- [ ] The v0 list is generated, not written by hand, and records its source and checksum.
- [ ] The stage:
  - gives the same output from the CLI and from the service (`tests/test_cli_service_parity.py`);
  - is covered for every status by `tools/stage_stub.py`;
  - leaves the default stage set and every existing report unchanged.
- [ ] `service/openapi.json` is regenerated. The new settings are in `.env.example` and `tool_limits.LIMITS`, and the contract tests pass.
- [ ] At page level, 300 pages stratified by page-classification's labels are annotated with written definitions. 50 of them are annotated twice, to measure agreement.
- [ ] Each level is scored:
  - accuracy and macro-F1, and the confusion matrix by group;
  - calibration;
  - latency, for text requests and image requests separately;
  - invalid answers and timeouts.
- [ ] A recommendation (stop, keep in the report, or propose a record field) is posted here.

## Boundaries

- No record field. A `document-type` field needs its own hub issue and AMČR's agreement (a class (c) change in `docs/shared_components.md`).
- `pages[].category` and `page_categories` stay page-classification's.
- The 15 PDFs, and everything derived from them, stay on the cluster and private (#5).
- Not part of the pilot release. No hosted inference.
