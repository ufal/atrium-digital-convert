- **Mixed documents:** 10 of the 15. Eight have `none` pages among `digital` ones (1–157 per document: plates, plans, photographs, forms, older reports), and two have `ocr` pages. Routing is per page; no document is all of one kind except the 5 fully `digital` ones.
- **Prior OCR layers:** two documents, both under `OCR_LAYER_DOCUMENT_SHARE`, so they are converted and only those pages are flagged:
  - CTX200914045, Acrobat Paper Capture, 15 of 36 pages;
  - CTX201900002, PDFTron, 9 of 34.
- **Vector-only pages:** CTX201100348 has 23 pages that draw paths and no text (plans, or text drawn as curves). They are `none`.
- **Page sizes:** the largest page renders to 160 Mpx at 300 dpi (CTX201303221), under page-classification's 179 Mpx cap. Inserted pages come in odd sizes (357 × 536 pt, 681.6 × 537.1 pt).

**Defect 1: words split at a font switch.** Fixed on 10-14.
- **Symptom:** "Ji č ín", "Oprávn ě ná", "P ř ílohy". Nearly 24,000 lone letters, almost all in 5 documents. They are exactly the Czech letters WinAnsi lacks; for CTX201400303 that is 12,029 against 138 for the letters WinAnsi has.
- **Cause:** Acrobat Distiller sets those letters in a second subset of the font. `extract_words(extra_attrs=["fontname", "size"])` groups consecutive characters by font before it looks at spacing, so every such letter ended a word.
  - Grouping by size alone splits them too: pdfminer derives the size from the font's descent, so it differs by float noise (12.000000000000007 against 12.0).
  - A second subset with another descent sits a few points off the line pdfplumber clusters.
  - All of this was reproduced here with pdfplumber 0.11.10.
- **Fix** (`api_util/digital_pdf.py`): pdfplumber's call stays. `_merge_split_words` then joins neighbouring words on one baseline when all of these hold:
  - same size to float noise;
  - gap within the word-gap tolerance;
  - the characters at the seam differ in font or raw size.

  The word takes its majority font. A superscript, a word after a space and an overprint in one font stay apart.
- **On `digital_born/sample.pdf`:** the same 267 lines, boxes, headings, regions, bold flags, columns and tables. 56 lines differ in whitespace only: "STRANA’S" for "STRANA ’S", "floor," for "floor ,", and a few emoji now attach to the word before them.

**Defect 2: glyphs without a Unicode mapping.** Fixed on 10-14.
- **Symptom:** a font without /ToUnicode gives pdfminer no character, so it writes the seven ASCII characters `(cid:236)` instead, and the decode check counted none of them. Affected:
  - CTX202100048 pages 33–39: 89–96 % unmapped;
  - CTX202200566 page 16: 100 % unmapped;
  - CTX202000362: 78 of 83 pages, where every ř, ě, č and ů is a `(cid:N)` ("poh(cid:3)ební").

  `make_fixtures.enrichable_pdf` had recorded this blind spot since 2026-09-25.
- **Fix** (`digital_ir.unmapped_to_replacement`, also on Docling's text): each glyph becomes U+FFFD. The existing rule then applies: a line over `GARBLE_CHAR_SHARE` (0.15) unmapped is `Garbage`, and its page is `garbled` with "garbled text layer: N of M lines are over 15% glyphs without a Unicode mapping …".
- **Outcome, by re-scoring the committed records:**
  - 18 pages become `garbled`: 10 + 7 + 1.
  - CTX202000362's other 68 pages stay `digital` with U+FFFD in place of the Czech letters (K4TEL, 10-14: share rule only).

**Seen, not fixed:**
- doubled glyphs on CTX201703695's four volume title pages ("ARC CHEOLO GICKÝ … R REPUBLLIKY");
- captions rotated along a landscape page's edge and chart axes, read one character per line (49 pages where most lines are 1–2 characters; some are columns of numbers);

**The stamp over a scan: the open case of 10-08.**
- **Measured:** with coverage ≥ 0.85 and ≤ 200 body characters, `tools/samples_report.py` found 2 candidates: CTX201703695 p67–68, each a page number over one image, on an odd page size.
- **Below the threshold:** CTX202000362 has 36 more `digital` pages whose only text is the running header, over two images. They stayed under 0.85.
- **What those 36 pages show:** a figure or a scanned text is unknown, because page-classification did not run (below).
- **Rule shipped on 10-14** (K4TEL's choice of the three options of 10-08: `none` in the record, the narrow form). The rule:
  - **When:** a PDF page whose only text units are running header or footer lines, with images covering at least `FURNITURE_ONLY_IMAGE_COVERAGE` of it (POLICY, 0.85, in `/info`).
  - **Then:** the page is `none`, with `needs_ocr`, the reason "no text layer under the page furniture: images cover N% of the page and its only text is its running header or footer ('67') …", and no page quality score.
  - Its page number stays in `lines[]`, and the schema invariant "`garbled`/`ocr`/`none` ⇔ `needs_ocr`" holds.
  - Coverage is measured only on such pages (`api_util/pdf_coverage.py`, shared with the report).
- **The design review's objection:** the hub schema describes `none` as "no text layer", while these pages keep a page number. The rule ships as decided; the hub's description could say "or only page furniture over a scan".

**Resources.**
- **Time:** CLI 1.2–34.8 s; `/reformat` 1.2–38.5 s; `/describe` with ocr-postprocess up to 74 s (CTX201703695, the stage 35 s of it).
- **Memory follows the text a document holds, not its size.**
  - CTX201202683 (4.8 MB, 178 pages, 31k lines): CLI 1.8 GB, server 1.9 GB.
  - CTX201703695 (156 MB): 1.2–1.4 GB.
  - Cause: the light engine kept every page's parse and every character dict until the end.
  - Since 10-14 it closes each page and keeps no per-character data. A synthetic 180-page, 1.3-million-character PDF fell from 2.7 GB to 0.2 GB (68 s → 51 s), with the same lines.
  - The samples are to be measured again. Until then the budget stays at about 2 GB plus the upload per `MAX_CONCURRENT_JOBS` slot.
- **Uploads:** `MAX_UPLOAD_MB` defaults to 200 since 10-14 (K4TEL; it was 50, which refused 4 of the 15). Measured on the real files:
  - 413 at 50, 200 at 160;
  - 429 `busy` with `Retry-After` at one slot;
  - both seed-digest checks pass.
- **The stages meet their own limits first.** The compact record reaches 14.2 MB (three more at 7.9–8.8 MB).
  - page-classification: 10 MB per part by default, and it also gets the whole PDF;
