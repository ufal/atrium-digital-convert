**New problems, fixed in the tree (R2):**
1. **The quality model reroutes born-digital text.** `route_page` sent a `digital` page to OCR when ocr-postprocess called more than `ROUTE_TRASH_SHARE` (0.5) of its scored lines Trash: 612 of the 1,981 pages.
   - **What it called Trash:** on the moved pages, 68,264 table cells, 13,074 lines under 20 characters, 1,581 lines of few letters (coordinates, find and photo IDs such as `DR-12982-18711;4990`), 60 dot leaders, and 7 prose lines. The moved pages are LINE_P 329 · PHOTO 187 · DRAW 42 · DRAW_L 37 · TEXT_P 16 · PHOTO_L 1.
   - **Why:** the model judges running text (perplexity, language ID); a born-digital table or caption is exact text that does not read as language. Its language ID even names digit lines `ces_Cyrl`, `ces_Tibt`, `ces_Arab`.
   - **Fix (K4TEL's choice of three):** only prose lines count: outside a table, at least 20 characters, at least half letters, not a dot leader; and a page needs 5 of them (`digital_report.line_kind`, `MIN_PROSE_LINES`). Over the committed run it moves 0 pages (1 without the minimum: a map of letter-spaced street names). The `/describe` report carries `prose_lines_scored` and `prose_lines_by_category`; when the old rule would have fired, the `nlp` reason says why it did not.
   - K4TEL's `issue5_trash_lines.py` asked exactly this; `tools/samples_report.py` now counts it in a "Quality model on born-digital text" table, and the script is retired.
2. **Lost letters went to NLP.** CTX202000362's fonts give no character for ř, ě, č and ů on all its pages. Under the share rule of 10-14, 10 pages are `garbled` and the rest `digital`, with `�` in place of the letters. Docling recovers none of them (the same 7,935 U+FFFD).
   - **Measured on the body text** (outside the running header and footer): 30 pages lose letters, a median of 19 % of their words, up to 25 %. 36 more are figure pages that lose them only in their running header.
   - **Fix (K4TEL: route them to OCR):** `/describe` sends a `digital` page to OCR when at least `ROUTE_DAMAGED_WORD_SHARE` (new POLICY limit, 0.05) of the words of its body text hold U+FFFD and a letter: 29 pages. The record keeps them `digital`. `layout.words` and `layout.damaged_words` show the count, with `stages=none` too.
   - A first count over all lines gave 66 pages; the 36 header-only figure pages would have gone to OCR for their header, so the count is the body's.
   - **The hand-off.** `/describe`'s `summary.reacquire_pages` lists the 29; `needs_ocr_pages` and `/reformat`'s record do not. Flagging them in the record would make them `garbled` (`digital_ir.text_layer_of`), against the 10-14 decision to keep them `digital`: open (Decisions).
3. **Paragraphs taken for headings.** `_assign_headings` took the body size over every line, table cells included. In table-heavy reports the cells' small type became the body, and every paragraph ≤ 160 characters a level-3 heading: CTX201202683 1,927 (Markdown `#####` lines), CTX201400303 2,824 (1,244 of them table cells). Prior-OCR pages gave headings like ",". **Fix:** only lines of `digital` pages, outside tables, furniture and vertical runs, count and can be headings. A simulation on the samples gives about 34 / 168 / 36 / 67 headings for CTX201202683 / CTX201400303 / CTX200914045 / CTX201703695 (Docling 192 / 166 / 49 / 177).
4. **Small caps split.** Word and Distiller set small caps as a full-size capital and smaller capitals: "O BRÁZEK" 212 times in CTX201400303, "Ž ÁNROVÝ" 39, "N EGATIV" 35 (CTX201600915), "T ABULKA", "P OPIS", "S EZNAM". pdfplumber groups characters by size, and the font-switch merge required one size. Docling splits them too. **Fix:** `_small_caps` joins an uppercase letter and capitals 0.6–0.9 of its size on its baseline, with no gap and no text rise. "P VI", "P IX-2" (feature labels with real spaces) and "V PRŮBĚHU" stay apart.
5. **Vertical table headers.** CTX201703695's catalog pages set their column headers at 90°; each character became a line ("K_číslo" as K / _ / č / í s / l o): 432 lines on 77 pages. pdfminer gives a rotated character its advance as its size, so grouping by size made a word of each; pdfplumber's `char_dir_rotated` cannot help. **Fix:** `_vertical_runs` groups such characters by direction and vertical baseline and emits one word per run, read top-to-bottom or bottom-to-top as set.
6. **page-classification's Hub loader** (sibling repository). On a first start the models are downloaded from the Hub, and `classifier.load_from_hub` left them on the CPU. In stage 61 the three ViT models (v2.4, v3.4, v5.4) failed on every one of the 533 pages ("Input type (torch.cuda.FloatTensor) and weight type (torch.FloatTensor)"), and the `all` ensemble answered with the two others, saying nothing in its answer. Stage 62 loaded local copies and used all 5: 21 of the 533 routes differ (17 `none` → `ocr`, DRAW → DRAW_L; 2 `htr` → `ocr`, TEXT_HW → TEXT_T). **Fix there:** `.to(self.device)`, and one warning per failing model instead of 1,599 errors.

**Phase B, answered.** The 36 header-only pages of CTX202000362 (coverage 0.58–0.64) are PHOTO 22, DRAW 8, DRAW_L 6, all routed `nlp` with their header as the only text. `FURNITURE_ONLY_IMAGE_COVERAGE` stays at 0.85.
