## 🧭 The issue (K4TEL, 11-12)

Two kinds of input: text only (TXT, CSV, TSV, JSON) and visually rich (PDF, DOCX, page images). This repository
processes every input: it extracts the text as is and gives visual cues a textual form; it selects the pages the
visual workflows (page-classification, the ATR) get; it does not decide whether extracted text is decent, which is
ocr-postprocess's scoring. DOCX is rendered and passed on as images and text.

## 🧭 11-25 — office documents never to the ATR; what a page shows

- **Decided by K4TEL for v2.0.0-beta (11-25):** an office document (DOCX, DOC, ODT, RTF) never goes to the ATR: its
  text is its XML, read in full (equations, SmartArt and chart text included). A page that holds only a picture is
  routed `empty`; text inside a picture is not read.
- **What a page shows:** each page's routing carries `graphics` (pictures of at least 24 pt each way, a repeated
  image under half its page left out as furniture; in a DOCX also charts, SmartArt, shapes and OLE objects) and
  `tables`. A page with neither holds running text only. `/reformat` returns the rendered PDF when a page shows
  graphics.
- **Which pages page-classification reads is ARÚP's:** asked on atrium-project#84 (11-24, updated 11-25). Until then
  the agreed route: the pages forced to the ATR.
