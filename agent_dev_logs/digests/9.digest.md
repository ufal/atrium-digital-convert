## 🧭 The issue (K4TEL, 11-12)

Two kinds of input: text only (TXT, CSV, TSV, JSON) and visually rich (PDF, DOCX, page images). This repository
processes every input: it extracts the text as is and gives visual cues a textual form; it selects the pages the
visual workflows (page-classification, the ATR) get; it does not decide whether extracted text is decent, which is
ocr-postprocess's scoring. DOCX is rendered and passed on as images and text.
